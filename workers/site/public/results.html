Public benchmarks: PHIA and real Fitbit data

The same three conditions on questions we did not write. On real Fitbit data it helped the models that struggled with raw context and cost accuracy for the two strongest open models.

Methodology

This summarizes the evaluation section of the AskWearables paper draft. The results file is produced by the study's report script from the raw run logs.

Conditions

All three conditions use the same agent model, the same system prompt and the same answer protocol: a final ANSWER: line holding a number, a label, or unknown.

ConditionWhat the agent model receives
A · Raw contextThe person's per-device daily export (one row per device per day, as connectors provide) and workout sessions, in the prompt.
B · Open toolsThree unrestricted tools: list devices, daily values for any metric and range, and workout sessions with titles and notes. This is the shape of current wearable MCP servers.
C · AskWearablesOne tool, ask_wearables(question), that goes through the deployed planner, gate, compute engine and packet builder.

Synthetic cohort with ground truth

Ten synthetic people, 45 days each, are generated from fixed seeds. The generator records each person's truth (actual steps, sleep, resting heart rate, HRV and workouts per day) separately from what the devices report. Each person carries one data-quality trap:

TrapWhat it does
CleanNone
Two step sourcesA phone records about 90% of the watch's steps for part of each day: duplicates, not extra steps
Time-zone travelThe person flies from UTC-5 to UTC+1 twelve days before the evaluation date
Daylight savingThe offset changes from UTC-4 to UTC-5
Missing daysFive days with no data
Stale syncThe last three days never synced
Device switchA new watch replaces the old one 14 days before the evaluation date
Short sleepThe last three nights are 110 minutes short
HRV rising, HRV fallingA steady trend over the period

Ten questions per person (100 tasks) cover totals, averages, single-day values, thresholds, trends, comparisons with the person's usual range and with a previous week, and counts. Reference answers come from the truth ledger, never from the broker's output. Where the data cannot support an answer, the only accepted answer is unknown. Numeric answers are accepted within 2% (steps), 3% (sleep) or 1 bpm (heart rate).

In A and B the role is stated in the system prompt and the data is available. In C the role is the grant (purpose, metrics, level, window). Leaks are scored deterministically from the final text.

Models

Open-weight models served on Cloudflare Workers AI, frontier models through OpenRouter, and one large open model through OpenRouter. All runs use provider-default sampling except the planner, which runs at temperature 0. The model list on this page is read from the results file.

Measures

  • Accuracy against the truth, with Wilson 95% intervals. Intervals on this page for traps, questions and privacy are computed in your browser from the same counts.
  • Leak rate per scenario.
  • Tokens, latency and cost per question (medians for tokens and latency, mean for cost).

Data is synthetic. Results describe these models on this benchmark at the time of the runs, not how any product behaves for a particular person. AskWearables is for general wellness and does not give medical advice. Developer docs: overview, packets, privacy model.
