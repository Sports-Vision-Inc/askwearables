\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Language models over personal health data.} A growing body of work asks language models to interpret
wearable data. Early studies serialized physiological series into prompts and showed that few-shot tuning or
fine-tuning lets language models make useful health predictions~\citep{liu2023fewshot,kim2024healthllm}, and PH-LLM
fine-tuned Gemini for sleep and fitness coaching~\citep{cosentino2024phllm}. Evidence on whether models reason over
the numbers themselves is less encouraging: language models remain near chance on zero-shot time-series
reasoning~\citep{merrill2024timeseries}, language-model components add little to
forecasters~\citep{tan2024forecasting} (for a contrasting result see \citealp{jin2024timellm}), a recent health time-series benchmark reports heuristic shortcuts and degradation with temporal
complexity~\citep{li2026hearts}, and a general time-series exam finds that models struggle with complex concepts
such as causality~\citep{cai2024timeseriesexam}.
PHIA~\citep{merrill2024phia} responded by giving a ReAct agent~\citep{yao2023react} a code interpreter and web
search, and released the objective question set we use. Later systems route queries to specialized
tools~\citep{zhang2026weqa} or combine data-science, domain and coaching sub-agents~\citep{heydari2025anatomy}, and
benchmarks such as SensorQA~\citep{reichman2025sensorqa} and WearableQA~\citep{lee2026wearableqa} probe question
answering over longitudinal records. PHIA and the multi-agent coach give the model, or the code it writes, access to the
full record and optimize for accuracy. \sys{} keeps the record behind a broker and measures disclosure, repeatability and
cost alongside accuracy.

\paragraph{Computation outside the model.} Program-aided reasoning delegates arithmetic to an
interpreter~\citep{gao2022pal,chen2023pot,li2024chaincode}, and tool-augmented models learn when to call
calculators and APIs~\citep{schick2023toolformer}. These gains reflect a structural limitation: transformers solve
compositional numeric tasks largely by pattern matching, and their errors compound with
depth~\citep{dziri2023faith}; solver-assisted pipelines cut error by large margins in engineering
domains~\citep{kodathala2026equations}. Data-analysis agent benchmarks~\citep{hu2024dabench} assume that the agent
writes arbitrary code over raw tables. In \sys{} the computation is neither performed nor generated by the model: it
is fixed and tested in a metric layer that the model reaches only through typed queries.

\paragraph{Privacy for language-model agents.} Contextual integrity has become the dominant lens for agent privacy.
ConfAIde~\citep{mireshghallah2024confaide} and PrivacyLens~\citep{shao2024privacylens} show that models which
state privacy norms correctly still leak when they act, CI-Bench finds that models struggle to judge the
appropriateness of information flows~\citep{cheng2024cibench}, PrivacyLens-Live finds higher leakage in live MCP and agent-to-agent
settings~\citep{wang2025privacyinaction}, and grounded evaluations extend to workspace
tools~\citep{guo2026agentciscope}. Mitigations largely remain model-mediated: PrivacyChecker prompts the model to
reason about information flows~\citep{wang2025privacyinaction}, and AirGapAgent uses a separate minimizer model
isolated from third-party input~\citep{bagdasarian2024airgap}. Data-minimization work formalizes the least revealing
useful disclosure and finds that language models struggle to predict it when prompted
directly~\citep{zhou2025dataminimization}, Rescriber lets users sanitize what they send~\citep{zhou2025rescriber},
while brokers that minimize before data leave the device~\citep{yu2026minim}, rewrite outbound tool
arguments~\citep{li2026toolminimize}, offer tiered disclosure~\citep{maeda2026puda} or encode enforceable user
preferences~\citep{kochar2026preference} move the decision toward the system. Violations grow with what is in context: contextual-integrity violations rise with the number of context
entries~\citep{fu2026ciwork} and accumulate across tasks and runs~\citep{mireshghallah2025cimemories}, agents disclose
context through tool calls that legitimate access does not authorize~\citep{dong2026claw}, and a data firewall that
projects information to task granularity cuts privacy attacks between agents~\citep{abdelnabi2025firewalls}. Most of
these works measure what leaves the model; ToolMinimize~\citep{li2026toolminimize} and Zhou et
al.~\citep{zhou2025dataminimization} also measure what is sent. For wearable agents we measure, per question, how
much of the record enters the model under each design. \sys{} makes the release ceiling deterministic: the grant,
the level and the window are enforced in code, while what is requested within that ceiling is still chosen by the
planner and the agent.

\paragraph{Authorization outside the model.} Indirect prompt injection~\citep{greshake2023indirect} and benchmarks
such as InjecAgent and AgentDojo~\citep{zhan2024injecagent,debenedetti2024agentdojo} show that agents cannot be
trusted to police themselves. Defenses that separate control from data range from the dual-LLM
pattern~\citep{willison2023dual} and design patterns~\citep{beurerkellner2025patterns} to CaMeL's capability-tagged
interpreter~\citep{debenedetti2025camel}, information-flow control in FIDES~\citep{costa2025fides}, application
isolation~\citep{wu2025isolategpt} and channel separation by fine-tuning~\citep{chen2025struq}. A parallel line
enforces deterministic policy at the tool boundary: Progent's privilege rules~\citep{shi2025progent},
purpose-specific policies~\citep{tsai2025conseca}, signed pre-action authorization~\citep{uchibeke2026oap},
budget-bounded capability tokens~\citep{suresh2026agentcap}, reference monitors validated on open-weight
models~\citep{aljumaily2026untrusted}, and aiAuthZ's off-host, identity-bound gateway with a hash-chained
audit~\citep{kodathala2026aiauthz}, extended to verifiable step-up approval~\citep{kodathala2026stepup}. Analyses of
MCP document how broad tool exposure enables exploitation~\citep{hou2025mcp,radosevich2025mcpaudit,hasan2025mcp}.
These systems decide whether a call may run; \sys{} also decides how much information an allowed call returns.

\paragraph{Reconstruction from aggregate answers.} Answering many aggregate queries accurately permits
reconstruction of the underlying data~\citep{dinur2003revealing,dwork2017exposed,garfinkel2018reconstruction}, the
observation behind differential privacy and its composition
accounting~\citep{dwork2006calibrating,dwork2014algorithmic}. Auditors whose refusals depend on the data can leak
through their denials~\citep{kenthapadi2005simulatable}. \sys{}'s daily release limit, which this study does not exercise, applies a simple form of this
accounting to agents that issue repeated trend and daily queries, with denials that depend only on the query
history (the number of packets already released), never on the data.

\paragraph{Repeatability.} Language-model outputs vary across runs even at temperature
zero~\citep{atil2024nondeterminism}, because of floating-point and batching effects~\citep{yuan2025nondeterminism,he2025nondeterminism}
and sensitivity to paraphrase~\citep{faghih2026samequestion}. Sampling-based remedies such as
self-consistency~\citep{wang2023selfconsistency} and consistency checks~\citep{manakul2023selfcheckgpt} trade tokens
for stability. Because \sys{} computes every reported number in code, the numeric content of a packet is
reproducible by construction; whether the agent's final answer is reproducible is an empirical question we measure.
