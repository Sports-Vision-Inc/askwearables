\section*{Ethics and Reproducibility Statement}\label{sec:ethics}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Ethics and Reproducibility Statement}

The study used synthetic data and the public PHIA, PMData and LifeSnaps datasets under their licenses. The real-data
analyses are secondary analyses of PMData and LifeSnaps, which their authors released publicly without direct
identifiers; using them is not human-subjects research as defined in 45 CFR 46.102(e); no re-identification was
attempted and no new data were collected. Question text and reference
answers from PHIA are not redistributed; the repository stores question identifiers and hashes only. The grants in
the privacy scenarios are illustrative, chosen by the authors. \sys{} answers wellness questions about a person's own
data and makes no medical claims: its packets carry a flag stating that they are not medical advice, the planner
refuses requests for grades, diagnoses or medical advice, and nothing in this paper is a clinical claim.

The broker, the synthetic generator, the question set and scoring rules, the privacy scenarios, the agent prompts,
the leak-detector audit with its hand labels, and the per-run records are available at \codeurl{}. Each record holds
the final text, parsed answer, tool calls, packet levels and gate reasons; records of the synthetic, real-data and
repeatability suites also hold every packet released. The pre-registration of the two steps after the study is in
the repository, which maps every table, figure and number to the script that produces it.
