\section*{Ethics and Reproducibility Statement}\label{sec:ethics}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Ethics and Reproducibility Statement}

The study used only synthetic data and the public PHIA, PMData and LifeSnaps datasets under their licenses. No personal health data was
collected, and no human subjects took part. Question text and reference answers from PHIA are not redistributed; the
repository stores question identifiers and hashes only. \sys{} is a wellness tool: its packets carry a flag stating
that they are not medical advice, the planner refuses requests for grades, diagnoses or medical advice, and nothing
in this paper is a clinical claim.

The broker, the synthetic generator, the question set and scoring rules, the privacy scenarios, the agent prompts,
the per-run results (final text, parsed answer, tool calls and packet levels; not the packets themselves), the
leak-detector audit with its hand labels, and the scripts that produce every table, figure and in-text number are
available at \codeurl{}. The commands are \code{eval/paper/run-final.sh} and \code{eval/paper/run-final-frontier.sh}
(runs), \code{eval/paper/run-final-retry3.sh} (retries of infrastructure errors), \code{eval/paper/report.py} (tables
and figures), \code{eval/paper/leak\_audit.py} (detector audit), \code{eval/paper/example\_packets.ts} (packets of the
worked examples), \code{eval/scripts/llm-planner-phia.ts} (planner study), \code{eval/paper/phia\_failures.py} (the
attribution of PHIA errors) and \code{eval/paper/make\_macros.py} (the numbers in this paper). Every result in the
text is a macro generated from the result files.
