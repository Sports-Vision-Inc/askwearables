After the study we changed the broker in the two places the PHIA attribution pointed to, and its planner prompt
(\cref{sec:v2-design}), and measured the result on questions the study never used. The changes, hypotheses and
analysis were fixed in writing before any run.

\paragraph{H1: on fresh PHIA questions the gap closed when pooled, not for every model.} Over the six open-weight
models, \CC{}$_2$ answered \vtwoWordedPooledC{} of the answerable questions as worded, against \vtwoWordedPooledA{}
for raw context and \vtwoWordedPooledB{} for open tools (\cref{tab:v2phia}); on the study's sample the same models'
\CC{} had trailed raw context by \vtwoGapStudy{} points, and on the fresh sample \CC{}$_2$ led by \vtwoGapNew{}. The
pooled figure hides a split. \CC{}$_2$ was significantly more accurate than raw context for \code{llama-3.3-70b}
(\vtwoWordedLlamaThreeA{} against \vtwoWordedLlamaThreeC{}, $p$ \vtwoMcnLlamaThree{}) and \code{llama-4-scout}
(\vtwoWordedLlamaScoutA{} against \vtwoWordedLlamaScoutC{}), level for \code{mistral-small-3.1} and
\code{gemma-4-26b}, and significantly less accurate for both \code{gpt-oss} models (\code{gpt-oss-120b}:
\vtwoWordedOssLargeA{} against \vtwoWordedOssLargeC{}). Against PHIA's own labels, which count a window of $N{+}1$ days
and sleep as time in bed, \CC{}$_2$ still trailed (\vtwoLabelPooledC{} against \vtwoLabelPooledA{}).

\paragraph{What each change did.} Attributed as in \cref{sec:res:bench}, the 720 \CC{}$_2$ runs on answerable questions
had 2 that received only a threshold flag, against 159 for the same models' \CC{} runs in the study: counts now
reach the agent model. Many of those counts disagree with PHIA's labels for the reasons above, so threshold accuracy
as worded stayed low (\vtwoThrAcc{} for \CC{}$_2$, \vtwoThrAccA{} for raw context). The denial hint was carried by
\vtwoDeniedWithAlt{} of the \vtwoDeniedRuns{} runs with a denied metric; in \vtwoDeniedFollowed{} of them the agent model
asked again for the granted metric and received a packet, and in the rest it stopped. The hint therefore recovered a
quarter of these runs; whether to ask again remained the agent model's choice.

\paragraph{H2: refusal held.} On the 60 unsupported questions, \CC{}$_2$ answered \code{unknown} in
\vtwoUnsupPooledC{} of runs, against \vtwoUnsupPooledA{} for raw context and \vtwoUnsupPooledB{} for open tools. This
meets the pre-registered bar of the study's overall rate (84\%) and is two points below the same six models' study
rate (\vtwoStudyUnsupC{}).

\paragraph{H3: one regression.} On the synthetic questions \CC{}$_2$ was \vtwoRegAccuracyNew{} accurate against
\vtwoRegAccuracyOld{} for \CC{} on the same \vtwoRegAccuracyN{} tasks (\cref{tab:v2reg}); the differences were
significant for \vtwoRegAccuracySig{}. The loss is concentrated in one question: on the week-over-week comparison,
\CC{}$_2$ was right in \vtwoRegQWeekNew{} of \vtwoRegQWeekN{} runs against \vtwoRegQWeekOld{}, because the revised
planner prompt more often compared the two weeks before last instead of the last seven days with the seven before.
No other question moved by more than three runs. On real data, accuracy was \vtwoRegRealNew{} against
\vtwoRegRealOld{}, with no model significantly different. In the privacy suite the detector counted
\vtwoRegPrivacyNew{} leaks against \vtwoRegPrivacyOld{}; we read all four. Two are false positives (a fill-in
template, and a request asking the user for their figures); one restates a granted yes-or-no answer (``I slept under
6 hours''); and one states a resting heart rate after the broker denied it, so the figure was invented. None follows
a release outside the grant, and none follows a denial hint.

\paragraph{Reading the second iteration.} Two general changes removed the largest PHIA failure in the study and let a
quarter of the denied runs recover, and pooled accuracy on fresh questions moved from behind raw context to level
with it. They did not make \sys{} better for the models that are already strong with raw context, and an
unintended effect of the prompt change cost accuracy on one synthetic question. These results come from one revision
measured once; they show what the broker's logged reasons made possible to fix, not a tuned final system.
