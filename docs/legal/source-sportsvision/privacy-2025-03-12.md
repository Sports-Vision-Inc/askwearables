## INTRODUCTION
Sports Vision, Inc., developed and published by Varun Kodathala ("Sports Vision," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use SV Broadcast and related applications (collectively, the "App"), our artificial intelligence platform for sports video understanding, including our website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").
Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our Service, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.
## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
### 1.1 Personal Information
When you register for and use our Service, we collect the following personal information:
- Name: To identify your account
- Email address: For account authentication and communication
- Age: To understand our user demographics
- Favorite sport: To personalize your experience
- Role: (e.g., fan, athlete, coach) To better serve your specific needs
### 1.2 Video Content
We collect sports videos that you upload to our Service. These videos are processed to provide you with AI-powered analysis and insights.
### 1.3 Device Information
We collect limited device information solely to determine which regional server should provide you access to our Service. This helps us avoid unnecessary cross-border data transfers and optimize service performance.
### 1.4 How We Collect Information
We collect information:
- Directly from you when you create an account, upload content, or interact with our Service
- Through Google authentication when you sign in to our Service
We do not collect IP addresses, location data, or use cookies or similar tracking technologies.
## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
### 2.1 Service Provision and Improvement
We use your information to:
- Create and maintain your account
- Provide, maintain, and improve our Service
- Personalize your experience based on your preferences
- Respond to your inquiries and requests
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance our Service
### 2.2 AI Model Training
We use the videos you upload to train and improve our AI models for sports video understanding. Before using any video for training purposes, we implement face masking technology to remove personal identifiers, ensuring no personally identifiable information is used in our AI training processes.
### 2.3 Product Development
We analyze aggregated, non-identifiable user data to better understand product-market fit and guide our development decisions.
### 2.4 Marketing and Promotional Activities
We do not use your personal information for marketing or promotional purposes.
## 3. DATA STORAGE AND RETENTION
### 3.1 Storage Location

- Data from users in India is stored on servers located in India

### 3.2 Data Retention
We retain your information indefinitely until:
