## Data exposure: health data delivered to the agent model per question

Characters of the user's health data the agent model received to answer one question (median): the whole per-device export in A (conservative: the workout list is not counted), everything the open tools returned in B, and only the packets in C. Ratio = median exposure divided by C's. Answers are the same questions; accuracy is in the table above. Volume is only half of exposure: what the model *could reach* differs too. In A the whole record is delivered; in B the whole record is reachable on request (any metric, any range, workout titles and notes); in C only the granted metrics, window and level are reachable, enforced by the gate. B's delivered volume is similar to C's; its exposure risk is in reach, which the privacy suite measures. B runs with 0 characters are answers given without calling any tool.

## Second iteration of AskWearables (planner v2)
